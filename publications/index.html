  1. ICLR
    Grounding or Guessing? Visual Signals for Detecting Hallucinations in Sign Language Translation
    Yasser Hamidullah, Koel Dutta Chowdhury, Yusser Al Ghussin, and 4 more authors
    In The Fourteenth International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR), 2026
    Abs Bib HTML Code

    Sign language translation systems can produce fluent output that is not grounded in the signing video. We study visual signals that reveal when a model is grounding its translation in the video versus guessing from language priors, and use them to detect hallucinations.

2024

  1. ACL
    Sign Language Translation with Sentence Embedding Supervision
    Yasser Hamidullah, Josef Genabith, and Cristina España-Bonet
    In Proceedings of the 62nd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (Volume 2: Short Papers), Aug 2024
    Abs DOI Bib Code

    State-of-the-art sign language translation systems rely on gloss annotations, which are costly and scarce. We instead supervise training with sentence embeddings of the target sentences, which take the role of glosses but require no manual annotation and are learned from raw textual data.

2022

  1. WMT
    Spatio-temporal Sign Language Representation and Translation
    Yasser Hamidullah, Josef Genabith, and Cristina España-Bonet
    In Proceedings of the Seventh Conference on Machine Translation (WMT), Dec 2022
    Abs arXiv Bib

    This paper describes the DFKI-MLT submission to the WMT-SLT 2022 sign language translation task from Swiss German Sign Language (video) into German (text). Instead of relying on features that ignore temporal structure, we present a system that learns spatio-temporal feature representations and translation in a single model, resulting in a real end-to-end architecture expected to better generalize to new data sets.
